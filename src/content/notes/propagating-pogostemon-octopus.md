Pogostemon "Octopus" is a stem plant that once purchased can be fragmented and grown in tanks rapidly.  As a stem plant, it rapidly absorbs nutrients from the water column and locks those nutrients up into the tissues of the plant itself.   Like most stem plants, Pogostemon can be propagated by cuttings.   It is highly recommended that when selecting a cutting for Pogostemon that you only select a length that will allow the plant to grow straight.  I have had extensive experience trying to grow plants with bends in them and most of the time I have found that these bends have for one reason or another significantly lowered by success rate.

To propagate Pogostemon "Octopus" first select an adult plant that will require you to cut it back (Picture 1).  This original adult plant is what we will call the "Mother" plant.   Select the designed length of the daughter plant and cut beneath the leaves of the base length of the daughter plant (Picture two).  Please note for this plant it really is not necessary to uproot the mother plant when taking a cutting.  

Plant the newly cut daughter plant into the substrate using a curved forceps (Picture 3).  I highly recommend using the Aquavitro variety as the quality of the metal tools have been first rate.  I have never regretted purchasing these tools and with minimal care they can last you decades.   Using a curved forceps grab ahold of the base where the  leaves branch out from the stem and position the plant where you would like it to grow  beneath the substrate.  The curved nature of the forceps is superior here to the straight forceps as it allows the substrate to cover the top sealing it in place.  Often times plants will rise out of the substrate if you use straight forceps preventing precise positioning of the daughter plant.  You wish to bury the bottom two leaves as if they were roots.  This helps anchor the plant while it regrows roots from its cut stem.

Once the mother plant is cut, a few weeks later you will notice the other leaves starting to produce side plants on their own. If you need more cuttings, take advantage of this to take and create more plants for your tanks.  Please also note that when you cut a daughter plant, don't leave that daughter plant out of the water for any amount of time.  Cut daughter plants are very vulnerable to desiccation in the open air.
