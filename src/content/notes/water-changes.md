summary: Water changes are vital part of fishkeeping, here is a guide to help you.
quickAnswer: Always use dechlorinated water, RO DI needs to condition using a
  product such as Seachem Equilibrium.
practicalSteps:
  - Fill a 5 gallon bucket with water from your tap.
  - Assuming your not using RO DI, dechlorinate with Seachem Prime.
  - Measure out 14 drops per five gallon bucket.
  - If using RO DI, use the appropriate Seachem Equilibrium.
  - Empty out the water from your tank using the siphon.
  - "Dump water on plants! "
  - Put the water into the tank.
