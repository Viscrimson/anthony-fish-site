Elodea or Anacharis is a plant that is highly recommend to anyone who is trying to learn how to keep plants and its succeeding in killing plants.  Elodea is pretty much bullet proof in that it readily grows even in adverse conditions.  When happy it rapidly grows which gives the aquarist feedback as to when they have made conditions better for plant life.  It can be grown both in substrate and floating in the water column.   Like all plants it takes nitrates out of the water and locks it up in its tissues.  Finally as a stem plant it can be purchased once, grown and distributed as cuttings to other tanks in your fish room making it a cheap and highly effective plant.

First select a single stem of Elodea.  Next decide how many plants you want.   Cut those plants according to the desired length.   I would recommend that you allow daughter cuttings to be at least one inch in length although truthfully you can make them smaller.  

Plant the bottom of the stem into the substrate if you desire a plant with a fixed point on the ground.   Bury the bottom most leaves in order to help anchor the plant.  I would recommend that you use a curved forceps here as the curved nature of the forceps allows you to place the plant in the precise orientation that you desire.   A straight forceps is never recommended for planting daughter cuttings as often times the plant comes out of the substrate before you can withdraw the forceps upon letting go.  

Allow to float if you want to provide cover throughout the water column.  Fertilize the tank, turn on the light, and provide CO2 if you want rapid growth.
